CLINICAL TRIAL PROTOCOL
Trial ID: NCT62780164
Title: A Phase IV Study of I in Rheumatoid Arthritis
Phase: Phase III
Sponsor: Bristol Myers Squibb

1. STUDY OBJECTIVES
Primary Objective: To evaluate the efficacy and safety of the investigational treatment in patients with rheumatoid arthritis.

Secondary Objectives:
- Assess progression-free survival
- Evaluate quality of life measures
- Determine optimal dosing regimen

2. INCLUSION CRITERIA
- Age 21-85 years
- Confirmed diagnosis of Rheumatoid Arthritis
- Adequate organ function
- Able to provide informed consent

3. EXCLUSION CRITERIA
- Prior participation in investigational drug study within 30 days
- Active infection requiring systemic therapy
- History of drug or alcohol abuse within past year
- Severe renal impairment (creatinine >2.5 mg/dL)
- Significant cardiovascular disease

4. STUDY DESIGN
This is a Phase III randomized study designed to enroll 400 patients over 36 months.

5. INVESTIGATIONAL PRODUCT
Targeted-Therapy-456 represents a new class of therapeutic agents with a unique mechanism of action.

6. SAFETY CONSIDERATIONS
Patients will be monitored closely for adverse events. Specific attention will be paid to:
- Hematologic toxicities
- Hepatic function abnormalities  
- Cardiovascular events
- Gastrointestinal toxicity

7. STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Primary endpoint will be analyzed using logistic regression methodology.

8. ADVERSE EVENT REPORTING
All serious adverse events must be reported within 24 hours to the sponsor and regulatory authorities.
